What we kept finding

Two HUGE opportunities. In every practice.

We are hired to mitigate risk and support strategic growth: sort out people, then process, then technology. We have done that work in hundreds of offices across Canada. Two opportunities came up in every single one. Both were worth solving, so we built them.

Opportunity 01Reactive to proactive · Sporadic to systematic · Chaos to clarity

Care, structure and progress is hidden.

  • Meeting prep, in-meeting discussion guide, service delivery, and follow-through sit in separate systems. Nothing gives the client one view of what is being worked on and what comes next.
  • Activities, documentation, and next steps get buried instead of staying visible and collaborative.
  • Services and support are delivered differently by different people on the team.
  • Between meetings clients have nowhere convenient to return to, and education reaches them without the context of their own plan.
  • The value is delivered every year. It is just not visible between meetings, so clients do not see it building over the lifetime of the relationship.
  • Clients feel well served and still cannot describe what their advisor does for them across a year.

Opportunity 02Guesswork to instructions · Exposed to protected · Crisis to clarity

The plan you make for everyone else does not exist.

  • Emergency access to systems, files, and accounts lives in one person's head, in random sticky notes, or in a hidden notebook.
  • Injury, illness, a family emergency, or worse. Your clients still need service the next morning, and nobody has agreed who contacts them, what they are told, or who handles their business while you are away.
  • Insurance, ownership, and estate details are disorganized and spread across places nobody else can find.
  • Your spouse, your key support staff, and your successor have never been given step by step instructions for the first days you cannot be there.
  • Succession planning covers the eventual sale. Almost nothing covers the first 72 hours after a catastrophic event.

"If something happened to me tomorrow, my team would be guessing."
